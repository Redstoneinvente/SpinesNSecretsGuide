Likely first buyer to test

Hypothesis

A nearby celebration buyer with a date in mind

Birthday and small celebration demand can be qualified by date, servings and design. A made-to-order offer can avoid unsold stock. Test within the owner’s actual service radius.

Alternative test

A small gift or treat buyer

Potentially lower ticket and more impulse-friendly, but requires batch-cost, shelf-life and demand evidence. Do not bake a batch in advance; collect preorders first.

Start with people and communities the owner can actually reach. Which segment is most likely to buy is not validated yet.

Why sales may not be happening

Current evidence supports an information gap more strongly than any particular marketing failure.

High confidence

Insufficient funnel data

Evidence: no verified reach, inquiries, quotes, order outcomes, costs or sales history were supplied. Test: log source → inquiry → quote → deposit → order and lost-sale reason.

Unverified

Offer or ordering friction

Could be: buyers may not quickly see what to order, price, location, lead time or next step. Alternative: too few relevant people may see the page. Test: one complete order offer; count qualified inquiries.

Unverified

Trust or presentation

Could be: mixed-quality images or limited proof may reduce confidence. Alternative: customers may prefer other products or have no current need. Test: use the clearest existing photo, real details and a low-friction deposit process; ask why lost if no sale.

Not yet assessed

Reach, targeting and price

Account-level insights and own costings are missing. Do not infer that TikTok, Instagram, price or product is the issue until the inputs are checked.

Sell before baking

One scoped preorder offer

Choose one cake already represented in the photo archive. State servings, included finish, flavour, order deadline, pickup/delivery area and price after costing. Make custom work only after a deposit. Keep a small menu until demand and margins tell you what to expand.

Cost before price

Include ingredients, packaging, delivery subsidy if any, utilities where practical, and time. Track contribution per order.

One image, several useful jobs

Reuse the same product asset for a product offer, close-up/detail, FAQ, ordering explainer, availability reminder and review request. No new cake required.

Simple order path

Offer seen→Clear product & price→Message / enquiry→Confirm date, servings & design→Quote + deposit→Bake to order→Pickup / delivery→Review + referral

Reply template: “Thanks for asking! What date do you need it, how many servings, and what area for pickup/delivery? This offer includes [scope] at [price]. Your order is confirmed with [deposit/payment] by [deadline].” Fill in only verified details.

Next actions

First 48 hours

Make the offer orderable

  • Confirm service area, capacity, lead time, costs, price and payment/deposit details.
  • Choose the clearest existing cake photo.
  • Write one specific offer and short FAQ.
  • Share only after facts are confirmed; log every inquiry.
First 7 days

Run one free test

  • Share the offer in one or two relevant channels the owner can access.
  • Cost: Rs 0. Record time spent and where each inquiry came from.
  • Success: a paid order with positive contribution; learning signal: 3 qualified inquiries.
  • If no inquiries after 30 relevant reached people/views, change the channel, offer or target.
Days 8–30

Repeat only what works

  • Compare qualified inquiries and paid orders by channel and offer.
  • Improve the weakest funnel step, one change at a time.
  • Ask for review and referral after a fulfilled order.
  • No paid ads or subscriptions until evidence identifies a solvable bottleneck.

Keep score with sales signals

Track weekly

Source · reach where countable · inquiries · qualified inquiries · quotes · deposits/orders · revenue · variable cost · contribution · time · lost-sale reason · repeat/referral.

Decision rule

A paid order with positive contribution beats a high-view post with no inquiries.

Likes and views are useful only when connected to profile visits, inquiries or sales.
